Our purpose

The British Candlemakers Federation exists to serve and strengthen the UK candle industry. Our objectives set out the six core areas through which we fulfil that purpose - from preserving the craft of British candle making to promoting consumer safety and representing our members at the highest levels of industry and government.

01
Preserving the Craft

To ensure that the skills and arts of candle making in Britain are continued, while embracing modern values and quality standards. The BCF is committed to protecting the heritage of British candle making for future generations.

02
Setting Standards

To lead and influence the setting of standards for candles made in Britain. We work with regulators and industry bodies to ensure British-made candles meet the highest possible benchmarks for quality and safety.

03
Research and Development

To encourage and facilitate research and development within the candle industry. We support innovation and the advancement of materials, techniques and sustainability practices across the sector.

04
Technical and Legal Advice

To advise members on matters of technical and legal requirements. BCF membership gives businesses access to expert guidance on compliance, helping them navigate an increasingly complex regulatory environment.

05
Industry Liaison

To liaise with other federations and bodies in related fields. The BCF maintains strong relationships across the wider industry - including European and international candle associations - to ensure our members benefit from the broadest possible network and knowledge base.

06
Promoting Safe Use

To promote the safe use and enjoyment of candles made in Britain. Consumer safety is at the heart of everything we do. We publish guidance, support education campaigns and work with retailers and manufacturers to ensure candles are used safely in every home.

These six objectives guide everything the BCF does - from the resources we publish to the partnerships we form and the standards we help to set. If you would like to find out more about how the BCF puts these objectives into practice, or how membership can benefit your business, please get in touch.
