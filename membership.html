Why Join

The benefits of BCF membership

Membership of the BCF gives your business access to a range of resources, support and representation that strengthens your position in the UK candle industry.

01
Industry Representation

Your business represented with government, regulators and industry bodies at home and abroad. The BCF ensures the interests of UK candle manufacturers are heard at every level.

02
Candle Safety Guidance

Access to expert candle safety guidance and resources. Stay up to date with the latest regulations, testing standards and labelling requirements affecting your products.

03
Members-Only Portal

Secure access to the BCF members area with industry updates, documents, resources and direct access to fellow BCF members and the BCF team.

04
Industry Networking

Connect with a network of leading UK candle manufacturers and suppliers. Share knowledge, discuss challenges and build relationships across the trade.

05
BCF Member Status

Use of the BCF member mark on your products and marketing materials — a recognised mark of quality and credibility within the UK candle industry.

06
Primary Authority Scheme

Access to the UK government's Primary Authority scheme through the BCF's partnership with Cornwall Council, providing assured advice on trading standards compliance.

Eligibility

Who can join the BCF?

Candle Manufacturers

Membership is open to any UK-based candle manufacturer, regardless of size. Whether you are a large-scale producer or a growing independent maker, the BCF welcomes manufacturers of all kinds.

  • Container candle manufacturers
  • Pillar and moulded candle producers
  • Church and ceremonial candle manufacturers
  • Private label and contract manufacturers
  • Artisan and independent candle makers

Partnership

Primary Authority Scheme

The BCF and its members are proud to partner with Cornwall Council under the UK government's Primary Authority scheme. This is one of the most significant practical benefits of BCF membership.

The scheme allows businesses to receive assured and tailored advice on compliance with trading standards law. This advice must be respected by other local authorities across England and Wales, giving members greater certainty and consistency when it comes to regulatory compliance.

For candle manufacturers navigating an increasingly complex regulatory environment, access to the Primary Authority scheme through the BCF provides genuine, practical protection and peace of mind.

What the scheme provides

Assured advice on trading standards compliance that other regulators must respect.

Tailored guidance specific to the candle manufacturing industry.

Greater certainty for businesses when dealing with local authority inspections.

Direct access to Cornwall Council's trading standards expertise through the BCF.

Available to all BCF member businesses as part of their membership.
