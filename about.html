About the BCF

Passionate about candles. Committed to the industry.

Who we are

The British Candlemakers Federation (BCF) represents UK candle manufacturers and their suppliers. Founded in 1978, the BCF has grown to become one of the leading candle associations in the world - with around 40 member companies whose combined output represents more than half of all candle production in the United Kingdom.

Our members produce some of the finest quality candles in the nation and worldwide. From large-scale manufacturers to specialist independent producers, the BCF brings together the full breadth of the UK candle industry under one roof.

"One of the world's leading candle industry associations - proudly founded and based in Britain since 1978."

What we do

The BCF exists to serve and strengthen the UK candle industry. We represent our members' interests with government, regulators and industry bodies - ensuring the candle trade has a strong, credible voice at every level. We promote the highest standards of candle safety, publish guidance for both manufacturers and consumers, and work to uphold the quality and reputation of British-made candles at home and abroad.

Membership of the BCF gives businesses access to resources, expert guidance, industry updates and a network of fellow candle professionals - as well as the credibility that comes with BCF member status.

Primary Authority Partnership

The BCF and its members are proud to partner with Cornwall Council under the UK government's Primary Authority scheme. This partnership provides BCF members with assured advice on compliance with trading standards law - helping businesses navigate regulatory requirements with confidence.

Primary Authority Scheme

The Primary Authority scheme allows businesses to form a legal partnership with a single local authority, providing assured and tailored advice that other regulators must respect. BCF membership gives you access to this scheme through our partnership with Cornwall Council.
