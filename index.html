About the BCF

Passionate about candles. Committed to the industry.

The British Candlemakers Federation represents UK candle manufacturers and their suppliers. With around 40 members, our companies together produce more than half of all candle output within the UK — making us one of the leading candle associations in the world.

We work to promote the interests of our members, uphold the highest standards of candle safety, and ensure the UK candle industry has a strong, united voice with government, regulators and the public.

The BCF and its members are proud to partner with Cornwall Council under the UK government's Primary Authority scheme.

What We Do

Our objectives

The BCF exists to serve and strengthen the UK candle industry across several key areas — from regulatory representation to consumer safety.

01
Industry Representation

Representing the interests of UK candle manufacturers and suppliers with government and regulators at home and abroad.

02
Candle Safety

Promoting and upholding the highest standards of candle safety — working with members to ensure British candles meet all relevant regulations.

03
Industry Standards

Setting and maintaining quality standards across the industry — helping members produce candles that consumers can trust.

04
Member Support

Providing members with access to resources, guidance, industry updates and a network of fellow candle professionals.

05
Consumer Education

Educating consumers on candle safety, quality and the value of buying from reputable British manufacturers.

06
Industry Growth

Supporting the sustainable growth of the UK candle industry — fostering innovation, quality and the long-term health of the trade.

BCF Membership

The benefits of joining

Membership of the BCF gives your business access to a range of resources, support and representation that strengthens your position in the UK candle industry.

  • Industry representation with government and regulators
  • Access to candle safety guidance and resources
  • Members-only portal and industry updates
  • Networking with leading UK candle manufacturers
  • BCF member status and use of the BCF mark
  • Access to the Primary Authority scheme
Apply for Membership

Who can join?

Membership of the BCF is open to UK candle manufacturers and their direct suppliers. Whether you're an established producer or a growing independent maker, we'd love to hear from you.

To find out more about membership eligibility, fees and the application process, get in touch with our team directly.
